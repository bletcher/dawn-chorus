Who sings when

Each bar spans a species' vocal activity — onset (5th percentile of detection times) to offset (95th) — in minutes from civil dawn (dashed line), taken as the median across the scoped period's mornings. Darker bars are sung more continuously; the tick marks the median busiest minute.

Cumulative call distributions

The empirical CDF F(t) — share of a species' detections that have occurred by each minute — averaged across the period's mornings (each morning a replicate). Onset reads where a curve crosses 0.05, median song-time at 0.5, offset at 0.95.

Occupancy across the morning

Species × solar-minute. Colour is the fraction of the scoped period's mornings a species was detected in each 5-minute bin — slide the time scope to watch the chorus shift.

Per-species table

Aggregated over the scoped period: mornings present, total detections, and median onset/offset/span/peak/occupancy (minutes from civil dawn).

Using this dashboard

Every chart plots a species' morning singing in solar time — minutes from civil dawn (the dashed line at 0) — so mornings weeks apart line up.

Time scope

Aggregate (Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly) plus the Period slider pick a set of mornings, and every chart recomputes for them. Slide through time to watch the chorus shift.

The charts

  • Who sings when — each bar runs from a species' onset (when 5% of its detections have occurred) to its offset (95%), median across the period's mornings. Darker = sung more continuously; the tick marks the busiest minute.
  • Cumulative call distributions — the curve F(t) is the share of a species' detections by each minute; it crosses 0.05 at onset, 0.5 at median song-time, 0.95 at offset. Toggle species with the chips.
  • Occupancy — species × 5-minute bin; colour is the fraction of the period's mornings that species was detected in that bin.
  • Per-species table — the same measures, aggregated over the period.

Good to know

  • BirdNET works in 3-second windows and doesn't separate song from call, so "span" is a vocal-activity span, not a song-bout length.
  • Charts count detections at ≥0.5 confidence; spectrogram labels go lower to show more calls. Many faint calls stay unlabelled — BirdNET didn't clear the bar.
  • Detectability depends on distance, wind, and the mic — keep the recorder fixed across the season for comparisons to hold.
