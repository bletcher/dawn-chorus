Dawn Chorus

Who sings when

Each bar spans a species' vocal activity for the chosen morning — from onset (5th percentile of detection times) to offset (95th) — in minutes from civil dawn (the dashed line). Darker bars are sung more continuously; the tick marks the busiest minute.

Cumulative call distributions

The empirical CDF F(t) — the share of a species' detections that have occurred by each minute. Onset reads off where a curve crosses 0.05, median song-time at 0.5, offset at 0.95. Robust by construction: one stray detection shifts a curve by only 1/n.

Occupancy across the morning

Species × solar-minute. Colour is the fraction of mornings a species was detected in each 5-minute bin — a map of the chorus filling in and thinning out around dawn.

Per-species table

Every species for the chosen morning. Onset/offset/span in minutes from civil dawn; occupancy is the share of active bins between onset and offset.
