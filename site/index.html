Who sings when

Each bar spans a species' vocal activity — onset (5th percentile of detection times) to offset (95th) — in minutes from civil dawn (dashed line), taken as the median across the scoped period's mornings. Darker bars are sung more continuously; the tick marks the median busiest minute.

Cumulative call distributions

The empirical CDF F(t) — share of a species' detections that have occurred by each minute — averaged across the period's mornings (each morning a replicate). Onset reads where a curve crosses 0.05, median song-time at 0.5, offset at 0.95.

Occupancy across the morning

Species × solar-minute. Colour is the fraction of the scoped period's mornings a species was detected in each 5-minute bin — slide the time scope to watch the chorus shift.

Per-species table

Aggregated over the scoped period: mornings present, total detections, and median onset/offset/span/peak/occupancy (minutes from civil dawn).
